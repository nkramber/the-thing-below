# Banned plot devices

Status: world file, v1. Owner: Nate. Started 2026-09-12 (D-140). Written in ASD-STE100 (D-10).

The world keeps the broad shapes that the genre shares, and it bans the specific plot devices of Final Fantasy Tactics (D-136, D-140). It also bans one device of Final Fantasy VII, because lessons follow the materia mechanic of that game (D-272, D-307). This file lists each banned device, its source, and the shape that stays.

## How to use this list

1. Read the list before you write an option, a story scene, a faction, or lore.
2. Compare the idea with each device, not with the shape.
3. Stop when the idea matches a device. Change the idea, or ask the owner.
4. Add a device when an interview option comes close to FFT (D-140).

## The list

| Banned device | Source | The shape that stays |
|---|---|---|
| A named brigade of betrayed veterans turned rebels under an idealist, which the story follows | FFT: the Death Corps | Unpaid soldiers turned bandit (D-127) |
| Demons that possess people through stones or relics they carry | FFT: the Lucavi and the Zodiac Stones | A thing below that answers spilled blood, known to a few (D-128) |
| A hidden scripture that proves a saint or a god a fraud | FFT: the Germonik Scriptures | Church leaders who know their god is a lie, and a player who can never be sure (D-137) |
| Stones that hold the evil, or stones that people collect for power | FFT: the Zodiac Stones | Waystones fixed in the ground that quiet the thing below (D-134, D-141) |
| A lead of noble birth who hides the family name to live as someone else | FFT: Ramza Beoulve | A lead of any birth who keeps their own name (D-267) |
| Two friends, one noble and one common, split by class, where one climbs to power through betrayal | FFT: Ramza and Delita | Class war among the cast and the factions (D-125) |
| A gem or orb that stores power | FF7: materia | A lesson on a page or a strip of soft metal, which teaches a form and holds no power (D-275, D-307) |

The session named each source from its knowledge of FFT and FF7, not from a reference it checked. Check a source against the game before a content PR cites it.
