## Findings

### P2-1: A damage trap gives no notice when it downs a fighter

Status: open.

Open at: `869cc599ee1c3c9e4fc4412a66f311af16b7788a`.

File: `TheThingBelow.Core/Runs/TrapRules.cs:160-167`.

Trigger: A damage trap reduces one fighter to zero health while another fighter still stands.

Expected: The map posts the down notice when a map harm downs a fighter and the party is not wiped. The notice text is approved in D-1241. The wipe notice covers a party wipe (D-397).

Actual: `HurtEach` discards the result of `MapHarmRules.Hurt`. The damage trap posts only `notice.trap_damage`. `MapHarmRules.Tick` posts `notice.fell_on_map` for partial downs from poison or bad air.

Consequence: The player gets no notice that a fighter went down. The HUD shows the empty bar, but it does not identify the event.

Correction: Track whether the trap downed a fighter. Post `MapHarmRules.FellNotice` when at least one fighter went down and the party did not wipe.

Regression check: Add a test where a damage trap downs one fighter and another stands. Assert that the damage notice and the down notice appear. The test must fail on `869cc599ee1c3c9e4fc4412a66f311af16b7788a`.
