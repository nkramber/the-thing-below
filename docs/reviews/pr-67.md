## Findings

### P2-1: The cost gate times the evaluator choice alone

Status: fixed in `ad83e20`.

Open at: `f92eb3e`.

File: `TheThingBelow.Tools/Evaluator/EvaluatorCostCommand.cs:127-131`.

Trigger: Run `make evaluator-cost` on the Steam Deck. Each sample stops its timer after `BattleEvaluator.Choose` returns.

Expected: D-961 requires a measurement of a complete enemy turn, with a 95th percentile no greater than 1 ms. The measurement must include the selected action's state update and emitted battle events (D-961, F-53, G-14).

Actual: The timer excludes the action application in `BattleTurns.EnemyTurn`, including strike resolution, healing, defend, or step. The command reports the evaluator choice duration as the duration of an enemy turn.

Consequence: A passing result does not establish the required enemy-turn budget. The performance gate can pass while the measured quantity omits part of the operation that D-961 limits.

Correction: `ad83e20` times `BattleTurns.EnemyAct` on a copy of the run. The call includes the action, its events, and the end check.

Regression check: `EvaluatorCostCommandTests.TheTimedTurnAppliesTheActionOnACopyOfTheRun` passed in `make verify`. The owner must still run `make evaluator-cost` in Release on the Steam Deck and report the 95th percentile at or below 1000 us (D-961).
