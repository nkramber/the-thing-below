## Findings

### P1-1: The 1080p pixel rule cannot be satisfied

Status: open.

File: `docs/decisions.md:708`, `docs/questions.md:329-332`, `docs/roadmaps/area-ui-input.md:71-75`, and `docs/roadmaps/phase-2-first-playable.md:248-251`.

Trigger: Fit the 1280 by 720 frame to a 1920 by 1080 screen, as PR-61 and the PR-41 review focus require.

Expected: D-568 requires the art at 1920 by 1080 to have no blur and no uneven pixels. OQ-105 must offer a method that can meet that rule, and the roadmap exit and review checks must be possible to pass (T-2, D-144, and G-16).

Actual: The scale is 1.5. Nearest filtering must map two source pixels to three output pixels, so the output widths alternate. Linear filtering combines adjacent source pixels, so it adds blur. The recommended two-step method first makes a 2560 by 1440 nearest image and then reduces it to 1920 by 1080 with a linear ratio of 0.75. OQ-105 itself says that this method adds softness. Each listed option violates either the no-blur rule or the no-uneven-pixel rule.

Consequence: PR-61 cannot implement D-568 as written, and PR-41 cannot prove the required result. The Gate 2 screen evidence can only pass through an unstated relaxation of an owner decision.

Correction: Ask the owner to choose the 1080p tradeoff. Accept filtered softness, accept uneven nearest-neighbor pixels, or keep an exact integer scale with bars. Then update D-568, OQ-105, the PR-61 exit tests, the PR-41 review focus, and the screen rule in the guidance files together.

Regression check: For displays of 1280 by 800, 1920 by 1080, and 2560 by 1440, state the exact source-to-output pixel rule and the accepted visual property. The selected rule must not require both equal pixel widths and no sample mixing at a non-integer scale.
